IACF Workflow Management: Tailored for Diverse Workflows

The IACF Workflow Management feature is not a one-size-fits-all solution; it's a versatile toolkit that empowers businesses to define and automate diverse workflows. Here's how IACF transforms the management of various processes:

1. Graphic Flow Design: Intuitive and Efficient

  • Drag and Drop BPMN Blocks: IACF's user-friendly graphic flow design interface allows users to effortlessly create and maintain workflows using the intuitive drag-and-drop BPMN blocks. Whether it's addressing non-conformance reports or overseeing the hiring approval process, visualizing and adapting workflows is now a breeze.

2. Decision Making and Routing Table Definition: Intelligent Workflows

  • Support for Decision-Making: IACF's Workflow Management supports dynamic decision-making. Define decision points within your workflow, whether it's for resolving production issues or obtaining HR hiring approvals, and establish routing tables to guide the flow based on real-time conditions.

3. UI and Background Transaction Integration: Seamless Connection

  • Linking UI and Transactions: Achieve a seamless connection between user interfaces and background transactions. IACF's Workflow Management ensures that all stakeholders, whether they are resolving non-conformance reports or participating in the hiring approval process, experience real-time updates.

4. Entity Explosion Against Workflow: Enhanced Detail

  • Explosion of Entities: Gain a granular perspective by exploding entities against the workflow. This feature provides detailed insights into how entities interact within the workflow, enhancing your understanding of each step's impact on the overall process, whether it's addressing production issues or managing HR hiring tasks.

5. Task Creation and Execution: Automated Efficiency

  • Automated Task Creation: IACF's Workflow Management automates task creation, ensuring efficiency across various processes. From resolving non-conformance reports to managing HR hiring tasks, tasks are generated based on routing definitions and decisions made during the workflow.

6. Task Monitoring with Graphic Flow: Visual Insights

  • Graphic Flow Monitoring: Monitor workflow tasks visually with a dynamic graphic flow representation. Different colors distinguish executed routings, making it easy to track the progress of tasks—be it resolving production issues or progressing through the HR hiring process.
