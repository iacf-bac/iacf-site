IACF Global KPI Portal & Digital Virtual Twin

2026-01-09

Transforming Manufacturing Through Intelligent Visibility and Control

Executive Overview

The IACF Global KPI Portal, powered by advanced analytics and 3D Digital Virtual Twin technology, delivers a unified, real-time view of enterprise manufacturing performance. By seamlessly connecting global operations, plant-level execution, and asset-level intelligence, IACF enables manufacturing leaders to make faster, data-driven decisions and accelerate their digital transformation journey.

Global KPI Visibility — One Enterprise, One View

The IACF Global KPI Portal presents enterprise-wide KPIs across all plants on an interactive global map. Executives and operations leaders gain immediate insight into overall performance, trends, and risk areas—without navigating fragmented systems.

Key Capabilities

  • Global map visualization of plant-level KPIs

  • Standardized enterprise metrics for consistent comparison

  • Instant drill-down from global to regional and plant views

This top-down transparency ensures alignment between corporate strategy and operational execution.

Real-Time Intelligence with AI-Driven Insights

IACF continuously ingests real-time data from MES, automation systems, and enterprise platforms. AI-driven analytics transform raw data into actionable intelligence, highlighting patterns, anomalies, and emerging risks.

Business Value

  • Real-time KPI updates for timely decision-making

  • AI-assisted root cause analysis and trend detection

  • Actionable insights tailored for executives, plant managers, and operations teams

The result is proactive management instead of reactive firefighting.

Intelligent Alerts for Rapid Issue Identification

IACF enables plant-specific KPI alerts that immediately surface performance deviations. Issues are visually highlighted on the global map, allowing leadership teams to identify and prioritize problem areas at a glance.

Benefits

  • Early detection of production, quality, or logistics issues

  • Clear visualization of impacted plants

  • Faster escalation and resolution across global operations

Deep Plant-Level KPI Analysis

Beyond high-level visibility, IACF provides detailed KPI dashboards for each facility. Users can analyze performance across production lines, machines, and operational domains to uncover improvement opportunities.

Capabilities

  • Plant-specific KPI dashboards and analytics

  • Historical and real-time performance analysis

  • Support for continuous improvement and operational excellence initiatives

From Global Map to 3D Digital Virtual Twin

IACF elevates manufacturing visibility through its advanced 3D Digital Virtual Twin, enabled by IACF Plant Studio.

From the global map, users can seamlessly transition into a fully interactive 3D model of each plant—creating a true digital representation of the physical factory.

3D Digital Virtual Twin Capabilities

  • Rapid 3D mockup of plant buildings, production lines, machines, storage locations, and material carts

  • KPI tracking at every entity level (plant, line, machine, asset)

  • Real-time visualization of production status and operational performance

  • Contextual KPI overlays directly on the 3D plant model

This immersive environment bridges the gap between data and physical operations.

Monitoring, Control, and Execution in One Platform

IACF goes beyond visualization. Through configurable integrations, users can monitor live production execution and, where enabled, send control commands directly to machines and production lines.

Operational Control Features

  • Real-time monitoring of machine and line KPIs

  • Status-driven insights directly in the 3D environment

  • Secure, configuration-based command execution to shop-floor assets

This closed-loop capability enables faster response, improved uptime, and optimized throughput.

Enabling the Digital Factory of the Future

The IACF Global KPI Portal and Digital Virtual Twin form a powerful foundation for enterprise manufacturing transformation. By unifying global visibility, AI-driven intelligence, immersive 3D visualization, and operational control, IACF empowers manufacturers to:

  • Improve enterprise-wide performance transparency

  • Accelerate decision-making with real-time insights

  • Reduce operational risk through early detection and alerts

  • Enable smart factories and scalable digital twins

  • Drive sustainable operational excellence

IACF turns manufacturing data into intelligence—and intelligence into action.
