Focus Areas

🤖

Agentic AI on the Shop Floor

Multi-agent systems with institutional memory, governance and proactive control — moving manufacturing from rigid automation to autonomous, software-defined operations.

🧩

IAC AI Agent Studio

A production-grade studio to create, configure and manage AI agents with their own purposes, behaviors and toolsets — model-agnostic across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Azure and Ollama.

🔌

MOM MCP — The Governed Bridge

A governed bridge between AI assistants and manufacturing applications (MES/MOM such as DELMIA Apriso): any trigger to an approved action, with a human sign-off.

✅

AI Test Automation — TestBot

Record it, write it, or just describe it — AI generates and runs application tests itself, closing the loop from requirement to verified execution.
